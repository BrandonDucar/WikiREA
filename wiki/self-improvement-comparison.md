analyzer-backed or runtime-tested result.

## Proposed DreamNet Reuse

The following is a public-safe design mapping, **not a claim that these
adaptations are wired, active or saving credits**.

| Existing Consumer | Proposed Reuse | Required Evidence Before Promotion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compiler and skill revisions | DSPy-style versioned baseline/candidate comparison | Separate final holdout, fixed metric, regressions, bounded search cost |
| Fish simulation branches | DGM-style lineage archive retaining unsuccessful branches too | Source/seed/clock identity and distribution-level outcomes, not selected wins |
| Agent acceptance and competency checks | Keep candidate self-reports separate from independent verification | Actual test commands/results, evaluator identity and narrowly scoped admission |
| Trade Invader paper practice | Compare paper strategies across chronological partitions | Fees, slippage, leakage checks, out-of-sample drawdown and failed trials retained |
| Swarm task handoffs | AlphaEvolve-client-style distinct acquisition/evaluation/submission states | Receiver acknowledgment, durable result readback and reconciliation after timeout |

No paper or simulation result authorizes live trading. No learning result edits
its own safety policy, spending limit or final test set. Existing mission,
memory, receipt and approval contracts should carry these fields rather than a
replacement agent platform.

## Useful Product Experiments

- Evidence-backed optimizer comparison packs for engineering teams: a fixed
  workload, reproducible candidate evaluations and honest failure reports.
- Read-only lineage and experiment dashboards: sell observability of actual
  runs, not simulated worker activity or guaranteed performance.
- Interoperability adapters that normalize candidate/evaluator/receipt records
  across frameworks, with protocol conformance tests.
- Public WikiREA research pages plus paid, separately authorized target-specific
  investigations. Source rights and confidentiality remain target-specific.

These are hypotheses with no established customer demand, revenue or savings.
The next useful proof is one bounded baseline-versus-candidate experiment on an
existing task with an untouched holdout, not an unattended self-rewriting fleet.
